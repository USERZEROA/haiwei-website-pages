Games

Sky: Children of the Light

Sky is my favorite game and the one that has stayed with me the longest. For about six years, from the beginning of my undergraduate studies in the US, it has been a place to spend time with friends—taking photos, chatting, or simply listening to music together. It feels like a familiar place to return to.

Ballads of Hongye

For me, this is the finest Chinese-inspired building game. Its detailed architecture, terrain, and engaging tasks make the act of building enjoyable in itself. I especially wish the train and boat systems had been developed further.
